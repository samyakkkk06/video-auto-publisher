PUT YOUR VIDEO CLIPS IN THIS FOLDER

- One file per clip, 30 to 45 seconds long
- mp4, mov, m4v or webv
- Name them in order: clip_001.mp4, clip_002.mp4, ...
- The publisher rotates through this folder, 5 clips per day

The system automatically converts every clip to 1080x1920 vertical H.264
which is what Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts expect.
