PUT YOUR WORD FILE (OR TXT FILE) IN THIS FOLDER

- Word (.docx) is supported, as well as .txt, .md and .json
- One paragraph block per clip, separated by a blank line
- The first block describes clip_001, the second clip_002, and so on

Example:

The camera pans over a quiet beach at sunrise, waves rolling in, and a
kite surfer catches a wave in the distance.

A crowded night market comes alive, neon signs reflecting in puddles
while people move between food stalls.

The AI reads this text and writes the Instagram caption, the YouTube
title, description, tags and hashtags from it.
